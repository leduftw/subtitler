Providers

azure-hybrid makes two passes and merges them

Pass 1 · the what

MAI transcript

mai-transcribe-1.5 in enhanced mode: the cleanest wording, but no word timings and no speakers

Pass 2 · the when and who

Fast transcription

Word timings and, by default, speaker ids, with the language auto-detected

align_text_to_words

Puts MAI’s words on the fast pass’s timings and speakers

group_words_into_cues

The same cue rules as every other provider

Two passes cost about $0.012 a minute, twice what azure-fast charges.

Outside MAI’s 43 languages the two passes may not line up, so azure-fast is the better choice there.
