Limits

What subtitler doesn’t do yet

One audio track

ffmpeg takes the file’s first audio track; any other track is ignored.

Anonymous speakers

Turns are marked with “- ”, never names. Naming them would need reference audio or Scribe’s speaker library, and neither is wired up.

Overlapping speech

Telling speakers apart gets worse when people talk over each other, with every provider.

No chunking

Audio over the provider’s upload cap is refused; a lower --audio-bitrate is the way under it.

No editor

There is no interface for fixing subtitles; the .srt is plain text.

No automatic QA

Nothing checks the subtitles beyond producing them; reviewing them is manual.
