The cue model

Four limits turn words into readable cues

84

characters at most in one cue

6 s

the longest one cue may run

0.9 s

a longer pause between words starts a new cue

1

speaker per cue: a new voice starts a new one

Every grouped cue lasts at least 0.5 s, and its text wraps at 42 characters a line.

With fewer than two words there is nothing to group, so the provider keeps what it has: a phrase, or the words themselves.
