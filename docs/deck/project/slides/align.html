Alignment is a diff of two word lists

Tokens are lowercased and stripped to Unicode word characters, including underscores. difflib’s SequenceMatcher lines up the two lists:

Where the two listsWhat align.py does
agree (equal)copies each timed word’s start, end and speaker onto MAI’s word
differ (replace)spreads the timed span evenly over MAI’s words, with the span’s dominant speaker
hold words only MAI heard (delete)fills the gap; takes the preceding speaker, or the following speaker at the start
hold words only the fast pass heard (insert)drops them: MAI’s wording wins

Aligned words never overlap. Grouping can extend a cue to 0.5 s, so final subtitles may overlap. Alignment uses only the standard library.
